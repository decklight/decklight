You found the lab note. The thocky sound, the default you are hearing, was tuned strike by strike against its own reference board. Each keystroke is a dark contact click bandpassed to 680-1250Hz and rolled off above 1.8kHz, gone within 12 to 17ms: gentle and click-forward, a pop rather than a boom. Under it sits a woody case thump that rings only 5 to 9ms, and here is the detail that sells it as a real board: the thump has two pitches. Most strikes land woody at 130 to 185Hz, and about one in five drops to a deep 58 to 85Hz thock, the way a real board mixes bottom-outs with lighter keys. A second, lighter click follows 35 to 80ms later, which is the key release, and loudness spreads ~4x between a grazed key and a bottomed-out one. Synthesized live in WebAudio.
